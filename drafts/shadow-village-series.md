Each part: a full video plus a Short teaser that ends on "FULL VIDEO LINKED BELOW" (set the Short's Related video to the full part).
Every part ends on a cliffhanger and a "PART N+1" end card. Playlist: "Shadow Village (Minecraft Horror Series)".

1. **This Minecraft Villager Knows My Next Move…**: the Seer's trades (torch, bread, sword) come true, then he offers your head; at 3 AM the door opens; the trade is SOLD. Cliffhanger: who bought it? (videos/shadow-village-1, videos/shadow-village-1-short)
2. **Someone Bought My Head in Minecraft…**: an emerald trail to the square; five hollow-eyed copies; one holds your pickaxe. Cliffhanger: `<Steve> my turn.`
3. **He's Playing My Minecraft World…**: POV as a copy; "you" break your own house; the Seer restocks. Cliffhanger: the music box starts playing.
4. **If You Hear This Music Box… It's Too Late**: the lullaby wakes Glitch; missing textures spread. Cliffhanger: `<null> i remember you.`
5. **I Escaped Shadow Village… (Finale)**: void holes swallow the copies; the desktop types "YOU'RE WELCOME."; twist: the Seer at spawn trades 1 emerald → YOU. End card: Season 2?
